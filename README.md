## Zuo Gou 苟左

I am an undergraduate in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University. My research covers embodied AI and robot learning, multi-robot coordination and communication, reinforcement learning, and diffusion models. I plan to apply to PhD programs for Fall 2028 admission.

[Homepage](https://tactino.github.io) · [CV](https://tactino.github.io/cv.pdf) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=XLtLIc0AAAAJ) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0007-9764-8231)

### Current work

**Perception sharing between humanoid robots.** Research intern with [Prof. Wei-Chiu Ma](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~weichiu/) at Cornell. Two humanoids share what they see in real time, so that a robot walking backward can climb stairs it cannot see using its partner's view. Manuscript in preparation, targeting RSS 2027.

**Diffusion models for protein design.** Research intern with [Prof. Ge Liu](https://geliu.web.illinois.edu/) at UIUC. Protein sequence–structure co-design in which the sequence is generated by continuous diffusion over learned amino-acid embeddings.

**[PlugRL](https://plugrl.github.io).** With Chenhao Lu and Zilin Kang; started in [Prof. Huazhe Xu](http://hxu.rocks/)'s group at Tsinghua IIIS. A wire protocol between RL trainers and environments, so that environments on other machines, in other languages, or outside the training cluster can still be trained. [Protocol](https://github.com/PlugRL/plugrl-protocol) · [Server](https://github.com/PlugRL/plugrl-server) · [step-ledger](https://github.com/PlugRL/step-ledger)
